Academic Guidelines — Click to expand

To achieve the best academic outcomes, students are encouraged to follow these recommendations throughout their studies.

  • Follow the teaching methodology, assessment requirements, and instructions provided by your course instructor.
  • Stay attentive to class announcements, deadlines, and academic expectations for each course.
  • Focus on developing a clear understanding of core concepts.
  • Practice writing answers in a structured manner using headings, bullet points, and relevant keywords.
  • Prioritize conceptual clarity over rote memorization.
  • Build a strong foundation in fundamental mathematical concepts and principles.
  • Regularly solve problems to improve analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Ensure a thorough understanding of basic formulas and their applications before progressing to advanced topics.
  • Invest time in strengthening programming fundamentals and core problem-solving skills.
  • Practice coding consistently beyond classroom assignments.
  • Develop personal and academic projects to gain hands-on experience.
  • Maintain an active GitHub portfolio and showcase significant projects on LinkedIn to support your professional growth and career development.
